-----------

installed so the Kit Wheel can show a kit rather than only name it. They are the
game's own artwork and are not ours. They are included for identification, and
sourced from the community wiki:

    https://robloxbedwars.fandom.com

Roblox and BedWars are trademarks of their respective owners. This program is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Roblox Corporation or the
makers of BedWars.

Removing the kits folder does not stop the program working — a kit with no
picture is shown by name, and any picture you choose yourself is used ahead of
the one installed here.
