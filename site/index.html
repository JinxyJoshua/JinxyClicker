Questions

Before you download.

Is it safe? My antivirus flagged it.
Jinxy injects clicks and keystrokes and draws overlays — the same things Windows watches for in malware, so some antivirus flags it on sight even though it's clean. If yours does, you'll need to allow it manually. Only download Jinxy from this site.
Will I get banned?
No tool can promise that — any automation is against most game rules, and you use it at your own risk. Jinxy's whole design is to not stand out: human-spread click timing instead of a perfect machine-gun pattern. That's the honest version. Decide for yourself.
What's free and what's Plus?
The click engine, shake, auto switcher and one-click Play are free forever. Plus unlocks Clip Studio, custom crosshairs, the keystrokes + FPS overlay, snap-tap strafe, custom fonts, the Display switcher and the aim trainer. One key covers all of them.
How do I buy Plus?
DM on Discord, pay the tier you want through PayPal, and once it clears you get a key. Open the app, go to the PLUS page, paste it, and it unlocks on the spot. No refunds once a key is sent.
Does it work on BedWars specifically?
Yes — Jinxy is tuned for Roblox BedWars, from the single left-click engine to the strafe keys and the hotbar switcher. It works anywhere you click in Roblox, but BedWars is what it's built around.
Do I need to pay for updates?
No. Updates are free, and Jinxy updates itself. Lifetime keys include every future feature at no extra cost.
